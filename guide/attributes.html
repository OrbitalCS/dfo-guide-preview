Attributes

Eight attributes, each from 0 to 100. This page says what each one actually changes in DFO, read from the game's own code. An attribute only matters where a formula reads it, and some read none: picking locks and pockets, and a spell's cost and power, use no attribute at all.

How they work

  • The live value counts. Formulas read your attribute with every Fortify, Drain, disease and piece of gear applied, held between 0 and 100. An attribute that falls to 0 kills you.
  • At creation each attribute is your class's value plus 0 to 10, and you get 6 to 14 more points to spend. You cannot lower one below what it rolled.
  • Each level gives 4 to 6 points offline and 5 online. DFO rolls the points once per level, so closing the window does not reroll them.
  • Default combat rules. DFO's combat runs on the Physical Combat and Armor Overhaul mod, on by default and always on online. Where it changes a formula, both are given.
  • The character sheet's to-hit line (Agility/10 − 5) is shown, but no fight reads it.

What to prioritise at creation

What each attribute buys, and who needs it most. Under DFO's default combat, several attributes do more than Daggerfall players expect; those are marked more than expected.

  • Agility, for every fighter. More than expected. The gap between your Agility and your foe's counts a quarter toward your chance to hit, two and a half times Daggerfall Unity's tenth, and counts the same way against foes swinging at you.
  • Speed, for every fighter. More than expected. Besides moving you faster, Speed adds an eighth of the gap to your chance to hit, which it does not in Daggerfall Unity, and makes you swing and cast faster.
  • Endurance, for everyone, early. Each level adds Endurance/10 − 5 hit points on top of the class's roll. Offline that is never paid back, so a low Endurance early is health you never get. Online, your maximum never falls below what your current Endurance would have given at every level with an average roll; at level 10 with 8 hit points per level that is 87 at Endurance 50 and 114 at 80. More than expected: above 50 it also lowers all the damage you take, 0.2% a point.
  • Strength, for fighters. Damage (Strength − 50)/10, doubled with a two-handed weapon; the weight you can carry; a heavy weapon's swing. More than expected: above 50 it lowers the damage you take, 0.1% a point.
  • Willpower, for anyone who meets mages. Willpower/10 on every saving throw against a spell. More than expected: above 50 it also lowers the damage you take, 0.1% a point. With Endurance and Strength, all three at 75 take a tenth off every blow.
  • Intelligence, for spellcasters. It sets your spell points and nothing else: it never makes a spell cheaper or stronger.
  • Personality, for traders and talkers. More than expected. About a quarter of what you pay in a haggle and a fifth of what you are offered, half of every court plea alongside Etiquette or Streetwise, and the start of how every person you talk to takes you.
  • Luck, for everyone. More than expected. At 75 or more your criticals come a third more often (skill/3 instead of skill/4), and loot reads Luck too: rare finds from half as likely at 0 to half again at 100, and better metal. It also counts a tenth of the gap to hit, both ways.

The eight attributes

Strength

  • Carrying: you can carry Strength × 1.5 kilograms, and the game will not let you pick up more.
  • Heavy loads: with the Roleplay & Realism mod, on by default, a load past three quarters of that slows you and drains fatigue.
  • Damage: (Strength − 50)/10 is added to every blow, from −5 to +5, and twice that with a two-handed weapon other than a bow. Under Daggerfall Unity's rules it is (Strength − 50)/5, with nothing doubled.
  • Swing speed: a heavy weapon swings slower, and Strength takes the weight off. This is DFO's own rule.
  • Fatigue: your maximum fatigue grows with Strength + Endurance.
  • Default combat rules also: above 50, Strength lowers the damage you take a little and helps your shield block.

Intelligence

  • Spell points: your maximum is Intelligence × your class's multiplier, from 0.5 to 3. A Fortify or Drain Intelligence changes it at once.
  • It does not change what a spell costs or how strong it is. Those come from the school's skill and your level.

Willpower

  • Resisting magic: Willpower/10 is added to every saving throw you make against a spell. The throw is held between 5% and 95%.
  • Default combat rules also: above 50, Willpower lowers the damage you take a little and helps your shield block, and with a class bonus against a kind of foe it widens that bonus.

Agility

  • Hitting and being hit: (your Agility − your foe's)/4 is added to your chance to hit, and your foe's chance to hit you counts it the other way. Under Daggerfall Unity's rules it is /10. Every chance is held between 3% and 97%.
  • Two weapons: with a weapon in each hand, the off hand is −5 to hit at Agility 50, a point better or worse per 10 Agility.
  • Default combat rules also: Agility helps your shield block, and builds most of the bonus a class gets from its weapon expertise.

Endurance

  • Health: each level adds a roll of half to all of your class's hit points per level, plus Endurance/10 − 5, at least 1. The level-up reads Endurance without its Fortify. Online, your maximum health never falls below what your current Endurance would have given every level, so raising it later counts back.
  • Resting: each hour of rest heals Endurance/10 − 5 more (see Medical).
  • Fatigue and breath: maximum fatigue grows with Strength + Endurance, and your maximum breath under water is Endurance/2.
  • Default combat rules also: above 50, Endurance lowers the damage you take, twice as much as Strength or Willpower, and helps your shield block.
  • Survival: Endurance sets how much drink you can take before it tells, and how much heat or cold before bare feet cost you.

Personality

  • Prices: with Mercantile, Personality haggles every price. It is about a quarter of the haggle when buying and a fifth when selling.
  • Talk: how a person takes you starts at Personality/5, and decides whether they answer and how well.
  • Court: a plea succeeds at your legal reputation + (Etiquette or Streetwise + Personality)/2.
  • Calming foes: Personality/10 is added when you speak a creature's language, and Personality/5 when you use Etiquette or Streetwise.
  • In DFO: online, Personality counts up to 100 at most in a haggle.

Speed

  • Movement: walking, running, crouching and riding all start from your Speed. Running adds your Running skill on top.
  • Swinging: at Speed 100 you swing 1.67 times as often as at 50. This is DFO's own rule.
  • Casting: spells cast up to a quarter faster at 100, and a quarter slower at 0. This is DFO's own rule.
  • Default combat rules also: (your Speed − your foe's)/8 is added to your chance to hit, and Speed helps your shield block. Under Daggerfall Unity's rules Speed does not touch the hit.

Luck

  • Hitting and being hit: (your Luck − your foe's)/10 is added to your chance to hit, both ways. Under the default rules, Luck above 50 also makes you harder to hit.
  • Critical strikes: better Luck makes them likelier (see Critical Strike).
  • Climbing: up to +10 to every climbing check.
  • Loot: Luck scales the odds of a rare find, from half as likely at Luck 0 to half again at 100, and lifts the metal that found weapons and armor are made of.
  • Food: with survival on, raw or spoiled food makes you sick unless you pass a roll against your Luck.
  • Luck acts only where it is named here. It is not spread across every roll.
