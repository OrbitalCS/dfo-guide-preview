A player guide for DFO Online, in preview

DFO Online is The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall rebuilt for the browser, with online play. This is a preview of a player guide I have proposed for its site: five pages for a player who has never read the project's code. Every fact on the skills and attributes pages was read from the game's own code. It is a snapshot of 2026-10-25, it is not official, and it may change before it is accepted, or never be.

  • How to play: getting the game data in, a first character, the first hour, what differs from Daggerfall Unity, where to get help.
  • Races: all eight, what each race screen claims, and what the race actually gets.
  • Skills: all 35 skills, what each does in DFO Online, and how it advances.
  • Attributes: all eight, what each one actually changes, and what to prioritise at creation.
  • Custom classes: the class builder, export and import, and three example builds.

The game itself, and its official pages, are at daggerfalljs.dev. The proposed change is the feat/player-guide branch of OrbitalCS/daggerfall-js-source.
